## 🚀 Featured Project

### [Winter Arc Habit Tracker](https://github.com/naitikkumar614-bit/winter-arc)

A full-stack habit tracker that works as an installable app on desktop and phone. Tick 10 daily habits on a 31-day grid, track weekly and monthly progress, keep streaks alive, and log weight and study hours with graphs.
